Why Sizing Requires Certified Technical Rigour

Unlike basic online calculators that simply multiply appliance wattage by hours, DEVICE PRO’s calculation engine strictly enforces Nigerian safety and electrical engineering standards defined in COREN and NEMSA guidelines.

kW vs. kVA Power Factor
Never confuse real power (kW) with apparent power (kVA). Standard AC compressors and inverter drives operate with a power factor of 0.8. A 4.0kW continuous load requires at least a 5.0kVA pure sine wave inverter to prevent overload trips.
Inductive Motor Inrush
Compressors, deep freezers, and borehole submersible pumps draw 2.5x to 4.5x their running wattage during motor startup. Our engine calculates simultaneous inrush headroom so your system doesn't shut down when the water pump kicks in.
LiFePO4 85% Usable DoD
We size storage based on 85% Depth of Discharge for LiFePO4 cells to ensure 4,000–6,000 cycles (10–15 years). Cheap tubular lead-acid batteries degrade after 50% DoD, providing half the usable capacity per nominal kWh.
Regional PSH & Heat Derating
Solar yield varies sharply from Lagos (4.2 PSH) to Kano (5.8 PSH). We add a 30% Balance of System (BOS) loss factor to account for Nigerian ambient heat (35°C+ panel thermal loss) and harmattan dust deposition.
