Privacy Policy & Data Protection

How DEVICE PRO Nigeria collects, protects, processes, and safeguards engineering, billing, and operational data in accordance with statutory Nigerian and international data security standards.

1. Overview & Statutory Scope

DEVICE PRO Limited (“DEVICE PRO”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates as a leading engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company and solar power hardware distributor in the Federal Republic of Nigeria, headquartered at 15 Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1 / Victoria Island, Lagos.

We are committed to maintaining the highest level of confidentiality, integrity, and security regarding the personal and commercial data entrusted to us by our clients, partners, and platform users. This Privacy Policy outlines our data processing practices pursuant to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), and all guidelines issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Statutory Compliance Guarantee

DEVICE PRO operates full data compliance oversight. We do not sell, rent, or trade client data to third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators under any circumstances.

2. Categories of Data We Collect

In delivering engineering sizing audits, turn-key power plant installations, and dispatching commercial-grade solar equipment, we collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, company/enterprise name, CAC registration number (for commercial clients), corporate email, phone number, and designation.
  • Site Physical Coordinates & Premises Data: Property address, roof type (corrugated iron, standing seam aluminum, concrete deck, ground-mount terrain), GPS coordinates, and electrical utility DISCO feeder zone (e.g., Eko Disco, Ikeja Electric, AEDC, PHED).
  • Energy Audit & Load Profile Data: Historical monthly utility bills, diesel/petrol generator fuel expenses, peak kilowatt (kW) demands, daily operating hours, and essential vs. non-essential circuit distribution schedules.
  • Transaction & Order History: Quotation identifiers, issued pro-forma invoices, equipment model serial numbers, warranty activation certificates, and delivery dispatch waybills.

3. Zero Raw Card Storage Policy

All electronic transactions on DEVICE PRO platforms are handled with bank-grade financial security protocols.

Zero Card Storage Guarantee

DEVICE PRO does NOT capture, process, or store raw debit/credit card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV/CVC security codes on any of our servers or databases.

Card-based payments and direct Nigerian bank transfers are securely routed through our Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)-licensed payment infrastructure partner, Paystack Payments Limited (PCI-DSS Level 1 Certified). Sensitive financial credentials are encrypted in transit via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) directly between your browser and the gateway. DEVICE PRO retains only non-sensitive tokenized transaction references and transaction outcome statuses for bookkeeping.

4. Engineering Site Audits & Aerial Assessments

For rooftop and commercial ground-mount solar arrays, our COREN-registered field engineers collect roof plane measurements, azimuth orientation, tilt angles, shading obstruction indices, and electrical service entrance schematics.

Photographs and CAD schematics of your property captured during on-site surveys are classified as strictly confidential engineering assets. They are utilized solely to:

  • Simulate PVsyst, Helioscope, and Homer Pro solar yield models.
  • Calculate structural wind and dead-load safety margins compliant with the Nigerian Building Code.
  • Design electrical single-line diagrams (SLD) and NEMSA-compliant earthing topologies.

5. Logistics, Waybills & Delivery Tracking Privacy

To ensure timely, secure freight of heavy renewable hardware (solar panels, inverters, and lithium energy storage systems), your delivery address and authorized receiver contact numbers are shared exclusively with our vetted haulage partners and dedicated fleet drivers across our regional transit hubs:

  • Lagos Central Freight Hub: Servicing Lagos Island, Victoria Island, Lekki, Ikoyi, Ikeja, and mainland industrial clusters.
  • Abuja & Northern Distribution Depot: Servicing the FCT, Kaduna, Kano, and northern states.
  • Port Harcourt & South-South Distribution Hub: Servicing Rivers State, Delta, Bayelsa, and eastern commercial centers.

Real-time tracking links (e.g. /tracking?orderId=...) are authenticated using strict order reference codes. Waybill receipts are archived securely to substantiate equipment chain-of-custody.

6. Your Rights Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

Under the NDPA 2023 and NDPR, data subjects possess robust statutory rights regarding their personal data:

Statutory Right Description DEVICE PRO Commitment
Right of Access Request confirmation and a copy of personal information being processed. Provided electronically within 14 business days of verified request.
Right to Rectification Update inaccurate, incomplete, or obsolete site or billing information. Immediate correction upon notice to our operations desk.
Right to Erasure Request deletion of personal data (“Right to be Forgotten”). Honored, subject to mandatory tax and statutory warranty record-keeping obligations.
Right to Restrict Processing Restrict processing during ongoing verification or billing disputes. Immediate suspension of non-essential processing during review.
Right to Data Portability Obtain your engineering load audits in structured machine-readable format. Delivered in standard PDF or CSV format upon request.

7. Cookies, Local Storage & Telemetry

DEVICE PRO uses lightweight session identifiers and browser local storage strictly to ensure site performance, shopping cart persistence, and security verification. We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Maintains equipment cart items, user session states, and CSRF security tokens across page requests.
  • Engineering Calculator Preferences: Temporarily caches solar load estimates (daily kWh, inverter capacity) during your active browser session so you do not lose audit figures.

8. Data Retention & Hardware Security Measures

Personal and enterprise records are retained in encrypted databases protected by multi-factor authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), and automated continuous backup cycles.

Warranty certificates, serial numbers of installed Tier-1 inverters, batteries, and panels, and single-line electrical schematics are maintained for the full duration of manufacturer warranties (up to 25 years for solar panels and 10 years for LiFePO4 batteries) to guarantee warranty enforceability.
