The Hidden Realities of Running Generators in Nigeria

Fuel cost is only the most visible portion of generator ownership. When full operational expenditures are calculated, solar emerges as an urgent capital preservation asset.

Fuel Price Inflation
With deregulation, fuel price volatility in Nigeria means your generator budget will continue to escalate. Solar electricity locks in your kilowatt-hour cost at zero for the next 15–25 years.
Eliminates Fuel Pilferage
Commercial facilities in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt frequently lose 10%–25% of diesel purchases to internal handling shrinkage and unauthorized diversion. Photons from the sun cannot be siphoned.
Zero Acoustic Noise & Fumes
Generators produce carbon monoxide toxicity, heavy vibration, and acoustic pollution (75dB–95dB). Solar systems with pure sine wave hybrid inverters operate in near-total silence (<30dB).
Generator ATS Synchronization
You do not have to discard your generator immediately. DEVICE PRO inverters feature dry contacts that automatically start your generator only during prolonged rainstorms, reducing engine runtime by up to 85%.
