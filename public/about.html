Our Founding Reality

Why We Rejected the "Market Vendor" Solar Model

In Nigeria, energy is the single largest operational obstacle. Businesses burn up to 40% of their revenue on diesel at ₦1,300–₦1,600 per litre, while homes endure fluctuating grid voltages from 140V brownouts to destructive 280V surges.

Yet, for years, the solar market has been plagued by "trader syndrome" — roadside vendors peddling mismatched inverters, rebranded counterfeit batteries, undersized DC cables, and zero electrical safety engineering. When systems fail after 6 months, clients are left stranded.

"We didn't start DEVICE PRO to be another battery shop. We built an engineering practice that delivers guaranteed continuous power, transparent sizing mathematics, and uncompromising electrical protection."

Zero Guesswork Sizing: Every quote is derived from measured kilowatt-hour consumption, inductive starting surges, and peak sun hours (PSH).

Engineering Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Technical Rigour

How DEVICE PRO engineers systems that operate seamlessly through wet harmattan dust, torrential coastal rain, and severe power quality disruptions.

01

Precise Electrical Sizing

We calculate running loads and inductive motor inrush currents (air conditioners, borehole submersible pumps, industrial refrigeration). Inverters are never specified at borderline capacity; our systems maintain continuous head-room for uninterrupted operation.

  • Detailed load profile logging & PF auditing
  • Generator ATS automatic micro-synchronization
  • Solar PV array sizing factored for wet season irradiance (3.5 PSH minimum)
02

Balance of System & Protection

A solar system is only as safe as its electrical switchgear. We enforce Class-1 DC Surge Protection Devices (SPDs), dedicated DC isolators, genuine copper earth grounding pits (< 2 Ohms), and double-insulated UV-resistant solar cables.

  • Dedicated AC/DC distribution boards with IP65 enclosures
  • Overvoltage, undervoltage, and reverse-polarity protection
  • Pure copper busbars and industrial crimp termination
03

Tier-1 Hardware Exclusively

We refuse to install grey-market or unverified clones. All photovoltaic panels are BloombergNEF Tier-1 rated, hybrid inverters feature pure sine-wave outputs, and our battery storage uses high-safety Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) with smart BMS communication.

  • Jinko N-Type TOPCon & Canadian Solar modules (25-yr warranty)
  • Deye, Growatt, and SRNE hybrid inverters with dual MPPTs
  • 6,000+ cycle LiFePO4 battery banks (10-15 year design life)
04

Continuous SLA & Support

Commissioning is only Day 1 of our 25-year partnership. We back every system with local service hubs in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, stocking genuine replacement circuit boards, contactors, and cells for rapid turnaround.

  • 24/7 remote IoT cloud telemetry & fault diagnosis
  • Quarterly on-site preventive thermal scanning
  • Direct local manufacturer warranty swap service
