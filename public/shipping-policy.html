2. Delivery Lead Times & Dispatch Schedules

Orders confirmed with cleared payment or signed turnkey contracts before 12:00 PM (West Africa Time) enter dispatch processing on the same business day:

  • Lagos Metropolitan Area: 24 to 48 hours via DEVICE PRO dedicated fleet vehicles.
  • Abuja & Federal Capital Territory: 3 to 5 business days for direct depot dispatch.
  • Port Harcourt & Niger Delta: 3 to 5 business days for direct depot dispatch.
  • Other Nigerian States (Kano, Enugu, Benin City, etc.): 5 to 7 business days via bonded commercial freight carriers with insured tail-lift trucks.
Weather & Road Infrastructure Advisory

During peak rainy season (June–September), regional highway logistics routes may experience minor transit delays. Our customer dispatch desk provides daily SMS and telephone waybill updates.

3. Heavy Goods Offloading & Site Accessibility

Renewable energy hardware involves substantial gross weight and volume. For example:

  • A standard pallet of 36 Tier-1 Canadian Solar / Jinko 550W panels weighs approximately 820 kg.
  • A 15kWh or 20kWh High-Voltage LiFePO4 battery cabinet weighs between 120 kg and 210 kg per module.
  • Commercial hybrid inverters (12kW to 50kW) weigh between 35 kg and 85 kg each.

Mandatory Site Offloading Requirements:

  1. Clear Vehicular Access: The client must ensure an accessible roadway capable of accommodating 3.5-tonne to 10-tonne logistics delivery trucks without overhead low-voltage wire obstruction.
  2. Ground-Floor Offloading: Standard delivery includes curb-side or ground-floor garage offloading. If equipment must be lifted to rooftops or upper floors, specialized crane services or rigging teams must be arranged or requested through our Turnkey EPC project desk.
  3. Authorized Consignee Presence: The designated client representative or site engineer must be physically present to inspect and sign the goods receipt.

5. Consignee Inspection Protocol & Transit Claims

Before signing the physical and electronic driver waybill, the consignee must perform a visual inspection:

  • Verify that pallet security strapping and tamper-evident seals are unbroken.
  • Inspect solar module cartons for corner crush, puncture marks, or shattered glass.
  • Verify that inverter and battery carton serial numbers match the pro-forma invoice packing list.
24-Hour Damage Reporting Window

If any transit damage is discovered, make a clear handwritten note on the driver's waybill copy (“DAMAGED IN TRANSIT - PALLET COMPROMISED”) and notify DEVICE PRO Logistics within 24 hours at logistics@devicepro.ng with photographic evidence. Transit-damaged items will be replaced immediately from our regional buffer stock.

6. Freight Tariffs & Free Shipping Thresholds

Freight fees are calculated transparently based on destination distance, freight cubic volume, and gross weight:

  • Free Lagos Delivery: Turnkey residential and commercial packages exceeding ₦15,000,000 qualify for 100% free delivery anywhere within Lagos State.
  • Interstate Haulage Subsidies: Major commercial projects in Abuja and Port Harcourt receive subsidized inter-depot freight rates when ordered as complete system solutions.

7. Warehouse Depot Self-Pickup Protocol

Clients and independent solar installers who prefer to collect equipment using their own transport may select “Depot Self-Pickup” at checkout or via invoice:

  • Pickup appointments must be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance to allow our warehouse team to palletize and inspect serial numbers.
  • Vehicles sent for pickup must have adequate cargo capacity (open pickup truck, van, or flatbed) with tie-down straps. Solar panels cannot be loaded upside down or transported on passenger vehicle car roofs.
