The 10-Year Battery Chemistry Truth in Nigeria

Why tubular lead-acid batteries seem cheap upfront but cost 3x more than Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) over a decade under Nigerian ambient temperatures (30°C–38°C).

Comparison Metric Tubular / Gel Lead-Acid (200Ah 12V) DEVICE PRO LiFePO4 (5.12kWh 48V)
Usable Depth of Discharge (DoD) 50% (Beyond 50% causes irreversible plate sulfation) 90%–95% (Full capacity safely extracted daily)
Expected Cycle Life at 32°C 600–900 cycles (~1.5 to 2.5 years before capacity collapse) 6,000+ cycles (10 to 15+ years of continuous service)
10-Year Replacement Frequency Must replace bank 4 to 5 times over 10 years 0 replacements (Single installation cycle)
Initial Upfront Cost (10kWh bank) ~₦1,800,000 (8x 200Ah tubular batteries + rack) ~₦2,800,000 (2x 5.12kWh 48V LiFePO4 units)
Cumulative 10-Year Hardware Cost ~₦7,200,000 to ₦9,000,000 (replacements + acid refills) ~₦2,800,000 (Saves over ₦5,000,000 net)
BMS Intelligent Communication None (Blind analog charging, prone to thermal runaway) Active RS485/CANbus comms with inverter (Overvoltage/Under-temp safe)
Weight & Footprint ~520 kg (Requires dedicated ventilated battery room) ~90 kg (Sleek wall-mounted or compact server rack)
