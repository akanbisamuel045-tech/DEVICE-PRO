1. Agreement & Commercial Scope

These Terms of Service (“Terms”) govern all purchases of solar photovoltaic (PV) modules, hybrid power inverters, lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) energy storage systems, balance of system (BOS) electrical hardware, and turnkey engineering EPC services provided by DEVICE PRO Nigeria Limited (RC 1849204).

By issuing a purchase order, paying a commitment deposit, executing an installation contract, or purchasing hardware via the DEVICE PRO web platform, the Client agrees to be bound by these Terms.

2. Pricing, Currency (₦) & Quotation Validity

All equipment and turnkey EPC quotations are denominated in Nigerian Naira (₦ / NGN).

  • Quotation Validity: Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in a written contract, official pro-forma quotations remain valid for strictly seven (7) calendar days from the date of issue. Due to global supply-chain dynamics, maritime shipping freight tariffs, and foreign exchange (FX) adjustments on imported Tier-1 hardware, quotations expire automatically after 7 days if unconfirmed by a deposit.
  • Statutory Taxes: Invoices clearly state Value Added Tax (VAT at 7.5%) in compliance with Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) statutory regulations. Withholding Tax (WHT) credit notes must be remitted within 30 days of invoice payment.

3. Engineering, COREN & NEMSA Standards

DEVICE PRO installs exclusively to the highest national and international engineering benchmarks. Every turnkey solar and hybrid microgrid project is planned, executed, and supervised by engineers certified with the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN) and compliant with the Nigerian Electricity Management Services Agency (NEMSA) Act:

Engineering Quality Commitments

Zero shortcuts. Our installations incorporate verified low-resistance earthing (< 5 Ohms), Class-II DC Surge Protection Devices (SPD), thermal-rated PV solar DC cables, and heavy-duty AC/DC isolator disconnects.

  • Earth Resistance Standard: Copper earth rods with conductive compound bonded to array structures and inverter chassis, verified with calibrated earth resistance testers to meet < 5.0 Ohms.
  • Array Fasteners & Wind Load: Aluminum rails and stainless steel 304/316 clamps engineered for gust resistance up to 130 km/h pursuant to coastal Nigerian climate standards.
  • Cable Segregation: DC cables rated 1.5kV cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) protected in UV-resistant conduits, separated from AC utility distribution lines to prevent induced harmonic interference.

5. Payment Terms & Project Milestones

Unless otherwise specified in an institutional EPC agreement, the standard payment schedule for turnkey installations is structured as follows:

  • Commitment & Equipment Allocation (70%): Paid upon contract execution to reserve hardware serial numbers from depot inventory and schedule installation teams.
  • Delivery to Site (20%): Paid upon physical arrival and joint waybill verification of equipment at the client's premises, prior to electrical mounting.
  • Commissioning & Handover (10%): Paid upon successful system energization, testing under load, verification of earthing resistance, and issuance of the COREN Completion Certificate.

For direct e-commerce hardware orders (unbundled inverters, batteries, panels), payment of 100% of the hardware and freight total is required via Paystack before dispatch.

6. Delivery Risk, Title Transfer & Waybills

All freight shipments are accompanied by a serial-tracked DEVICE PRO Goods Delivery Waybill.

  • Passage of Risk: Risk of loss or damage to equipment transfers to the Client immediately upon physical delivery and signing of the waybill at the designated site (or upon handover to a client-appointed third-party logistics driver at our warehouse).
  • Title & Ownership: Full legal title and ownership of hardware remains with DEVICE PRO until all invoices relating to the equipment have been settled in 100% cleared funds.
  • Damage Inspection: The consignee must inspect pallet wraps, panel glass, and battery casings for transit shock before signing the waybill. Any visible damage must be endorsed on the delivery slip and reported to DEVICE PRO within 24 hours.

7. Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

DEVICE PRO is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses, including lost business profits or commercial downtime, resulting from utility grid failure or equipment outages.

Warranties are voided in cases of unauthorized modifications, tampering with manufacturer seals, severe direct lightning strikes exceeding certified Class-II surge arrestor ratings, or mechanical damage caused by external force.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

The parties agree that any dispute that cannot be resolved amicably within thirty (30) days of formal notification shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Lagos, Nigeria, in accordance with the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023. The courts of Lagos State retain supervisory jurisdiction.
