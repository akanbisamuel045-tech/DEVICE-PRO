### Specific Requirements for Your Quotation

To enable our Procurement and Finance Committees to evaluate your submission objectively and compliantly, your formal quotation must satisfy the following criteria:

1. **Exact SKU Matching & Specifications:**
   - Please review Columns A through F of the attached matrix, which contain the exact DEVICE PRO product code, product name, brand, model rating, and technical specifications.
   - For every item you stock, please provide your **Supplier SKU / Model Ref** in Column H and confirm compatibility with the requested technical parameters.

2. **Explicit Disclosure of Substitutions (No Silent Substitutions):**
   - If an exact requested model is unavailable, you are welcome to propose an equivalent or superior alternative.
   - **Crucial Rule:** Any alternative must be clearly and explicitly identified as a **"SUBSTITUTION"** in Column O (*Quotation Ref / Notes*), accompanied by the exact manufacturer, model number, and a technical datasheet. **Substitutions must never be silently quoted against our reference SKU.**

3. **Currency (Nigerian Naira Only):**
   - In accordance with our financial operating controls, all unit prices must be quoted strictly in **Nigerian Naira (NGN)** in Column I.
   - Foreign currency quotes (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY) or foreign-exchange indexed prices cannot be ingested into our baseline reconciliation system and will be marked unsupported.

4. **Quotation Date & Price Validity Window:**
   - Please state the formal quotation date (in `YYYY-MM-DD` format) in Column K, and the explicit quotation expiry date in Column L.
   - DEVICE PRO maintains a strict **30-calendar-day price freshness window**. We request a minimum price validity period of 30 days from the date of issue to accommodate project planning and procurement scheduling.

5. **Stock Availability & Delivery Lead Times:**
   - In Column M, please specify the immediate stock quantity available at your Nigerian warehouse(s) and the estimated delivery lead time for both immediate stock and forward replenishment orders.

6. **Itemized Taxes, Freight, and Delivery Logistics:**
   - Please specify whether quoted unit prices are inclusive or exclusive of 7.5% Value Added Tax (VAT).
   - If delivery charges to our central logistics depots in Lagos (Lekki / Ikeja) and Abuja (CBD / Gwarimpa) are separate, please itemize freight charges and handling fees clearly on your official quotation schedule.

7. **Warranty Terms & Local RMA Support:**
   - In Column N, specify the manufacturer warranty duration for each equipment category (e.g., 25/30 years for panels, 5/10 years for hybrid inverters and LiFePO4 batteries).
   - Please confirm that warranty claims and replacement hardware (RMA) are serviced directly through your accredited Nigerian service hubs.

8. **Product Authenticity & Distributor Authorization Credentials:**
   - As an accredited solar engineering firm, DEVICE PRO procures exclusively genuine hardware backed by manufacturer serial tracking.
   - Please include with your submission verifiable proof of your distributor credentials (e.g., official manufacturer authorization certificate, registered partner listing, or direct factory import documentation).

9. **Corporate Identification:**
   - Please provide your company profile, Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration certificate / RC number, Tax Identification Number (TIN), and registered office address.
